Before Intentionaut I led product and design at [StepStone](https://intentionaut.com/case-studies/stepstone/), ran product for AI, data platforms and machine learning at Sainsbury's, and was the first design hire at [Cazoo](https://intentionaut.com/case-studies/cazoo/).

## Two ways to work with me

**[Fractional and interim product leadership](https://intentionaut.com/fractional/).** For teams building AI products where nobody senior owns product direction. I lead from inside the team: I make the calls, coach the people doing the work and answer for delivery.

**[Product building](https://intentionaut.com/forward-deployed/).** For teams with a clear problem and nobody free to build the answer. I work inside your repo and stack, with your data and constraints. At the end you have a prototype that settles the question or software ready for your team to own, and I tell you which one it is. Your team keeps product authority throughout.

## What I'm building

**Dualtrack** (private for now). A product team for Claude Code. Each run reads the product, proposes changes with a reason and an expected outcome, and builds what the owner picks. A second agent reviews each change, and anything a visitor would see waits for the owner. Small changes can merge on their own only when the owner switches that on and a script confirms every check has passed.

**[Kernic](https://github.com/intentionaut/kernic).** A free, MIT-licensed command-line tool that builds a design system in the terminal (palette, type, spacing, motion) and exports it as CSS, Tailwind v4, W3C design tokens, a shadcn theme or a `DESIGN.md` that coding agents read.

**[Familiar](https://github.com/intentionaut/familiar).** A writing companion that starts from what you built and said. It interviews you, drafts in your voice from your own material, and returns an editor's report on your draft. Anything it cannot source stays as a bracket for you to fill.

**[Step-Free London](https://intentionaut.com/step-free-london/)** ([code](https://github.com/intentionaut/step-free-london)). Maps which tube stations disabled Londoners can use against where they live, by borough, from TfL, OpenStreetMap and 2021 Census data.
